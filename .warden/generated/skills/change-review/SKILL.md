# change-review

Review a change against its requirements.

## Upholds

- review-gate-first: A change reaches a reviewer only after its new tests pass on the change and fail on its base.

## Steps

1. `warden check --rule review-gate --tier boundary --base <base>`
2. `warden review brief --capability change-review --base <base> --head <head> --requirements <paths>`
3. `warden review triage --capability change-review --base <base> --head <head> --requirements <paths> --report <report>`

## Use this instead of

- dispatching a reviewer before the gate passes
- reading a review report and choosing what to do by hand

When a step fails, do its printed Next action, then run the step again. Fix the code, never the check.
